# Contributing to The Payments PM Handbook

Thank you for helping make payments knowledge more accessible and accurate.

## Contributions we want

- Correct a technical error with a supporting **primary source** (e.g., scheme, standards body, regulator, PSP, issuer processor documentation).
- Explain a payment-system concept from the perspective of a PM decision.
- Submit a Mermaid sequence diagram or a clearly hypothetical failure scenario.
- Add a provider-neutral case study with synthetic data, trade-offs, and acceptance criteria.
- Suggest a new chapter, with intended audience and three learning outcomes.

## Requirements for a new chapter

1. Begin with **learning objectives** and a short example problem.
2. Separate **fact**, **provider-specific implementation**, and **PM judgment**.
3. Define terms before using acronyms.
4. Show what the PM owns, influences, and must escalate to a responsible specialist.
5. Include one diagram, worked example, or decision table.
6. End with three or more practical exercises and sources.
7. Never include real card numbers, credentials, customer data, employer secrets, or nonpublic scheme materials.

## Editorial standards

- Prefer `authorization` in prose and preserve `authorisation` when quoting provider status names.
- Use **attempt-based** and **unique-order-based** metrics explicitly; don't interchange their denominators.
- Do not imply that a technical timeout proves a card payment failed.
- Do not equate authorization with capture, settlement, or merchant payout.
- Do not present a bank, scheme, regulatory or provider practice as universal unless a source supports it.
- Keep commercial vendor examples factual, non-promotional, and sourced.
- Mark hypothetical metrics, diagrams and pseudocode as illustrative.

Propose substantial changes with an issue first. Corrections with supporting sources are always welcome.
